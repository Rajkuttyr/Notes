- **PAT:** Profit after tax. Shows the company’s reported bottom-line earnings.
- **EPS:** Earnings attributable to ordinary shareholders per share. Useful for understanding earnings per share and calculating P/E.

### **What the ratios tell me**

- **ROE:** How effectively the company generates profit relative to shareholders’ equity.
- **ROCE:** How effectively the company generates operating returns relative to capital employed.
- **Operating profit margin:** How much operating profit it earns per ₹100 of revenue.
- **Net profit margin:** How much net profit it earns per ₹100 of revenue.
- **Asset turnover:** How efficiently the asset base generates revenue.
- **Interest coverage:** How comfortably operating earnings cover interest expense.
