# Money Laundering

**Money laundering** is the process of making illegally earned (“dirty”) money appear legitimate. Its purpose is to hide the true source, ownership, and movement of criminal proceeds—for example, money from drug trafficking.

## The 3 Stages of Money Laundering

### 1. Placement

Introducing illicit cash into the financial system.

Examples:

- Depositing cash into bank accounts
- Using casinos, shops, or businesses
- Breaking one large transaction into many smaller transactions to avoid attention

### 2. Layering

Moving money through multiple transactions, accounts, or assets to make its origin difficult to trace.

Examples:

- Transferring funds between different bank accounts
- Moving money across countries
- Buying and selling real estate or other assets

**Purpose:** create a confusing trail that hides the original criminal source.

### 3. Integration

Reintroducing the money into the legitimate economy so it appears lawful.

Examples:

- Investing in businesses
- Purchasing property
- Reporting criminal proceeds as legitimate business income

> In short: **dirty money → placement → layering → integration → seemingly legitimate money**
